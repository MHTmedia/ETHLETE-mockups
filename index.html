ETHLETE was formulated with Ivy-League scientists to fuel performance. Each stick combines clinical doses of electrolytes, creatine, and essential vitamins (B6, B12, C) with zero artificial colors or fillers to deliver hydration that supports muscle recovery, mental focus, and cellular hydration.
  • Sodium (Himalayan salt) for rapid fluid absorption
  • Potassium citrate to prevent cramps and support heart function
  • Magnesium glycinate for muscle relaxation and energy production
 Creatine isn’t just for bodybuilders. It’s one of the most researched performance ingredients in the world. How to drink it

Mix one stick with 16–20 oz of cold water and shake or stir until dissolved.

Use ETHLETE any time your body needs optimized hydration. We recommend 1–2x daily:

  • Before training to prime your cells.
  • During workouts to sustain energy and reduce fatigue.
  • After exercise or travel to recover faster.
  • Or daily, as your upgraded alternative to plain water.
